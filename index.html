Materials

What it's made of

Not sure what to pick? Tell us what the part has to carry and we choose for you. Panel prices come with calculated weight and stiffness.

  • Carbon fiber. Twill weave. Stiff and light.
  • Carbon prepreg. Lighter and stronger, with a cleaner finish.
  • Fiberglass. Tough, and lets radio signals through.
  • Carbon and glass. Carbon stiffness with glass toughness.
  • Foam core. For stiff, light sandwich panels.
  • Finish. Matte, gloss on one side, or clear coat.
