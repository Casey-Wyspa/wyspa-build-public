Questions

Good to know

What files can I send?

Drawings as PDF, DXF or DWG. 3D models as STEP, STL or IGES. Sketches and photos work too. Up to 10 files, 25 MB in all. Bigger than that? Send the form without them and we'll email you an upload link.

My part isn't designed yet. Can you help?

Yes. Tell us what it has to do: the size, the loads, what it bolts to. We quote a design stage at a fixed fee, then a fixed price to build it.

Why do only flat panels get an instant price?

A flat panel follows standard rules, so we can price it as you type. Rotors and molded parts need a mold and some design work, so a person prices them.

What materials do you use?

Carbon fiber twill, carbon prepreg cured in an autoclave, fiberglass (tough, and lets radio signals through), and carbon with glass. Foam core for stiff, light panels. Honeycomb on request. Gloss on the mold side, peel-ply texture on the other. Not sure what to pick? Tell us what the part has to carry and we choose.

How close are the holes and edges to my drawing?

Flat panels are cut to ±0.5 mm. The smallest hole is 7.5 mm across.

How do I pay?

For a quoted order: 50% deposit to start, and the balance before we ship, by card or bank transfer. Paying by PO? Send the request and tell us in the notes.

Is shipping included?

No. We email the shipping price before we ship.
